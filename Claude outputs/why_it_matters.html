Section 1 — Why this project

Why Ridhi Block Print needed this

Before

No visibility

Revenue, conversion rate, cart drop-off and quilt order activity all lived only inside Shopify's own screens — no single place to see store health at a glance, and no alerts when something broke.

The risk

Problems go unnoticed

A checkout issue, a stalled product, or a payment failure could run for hours before anyone noticed — directly costing sales, with no early-warning system in place.

Now

One live dashboard

Real-time KPIs, automatic alarms, rule-based recommendations, and industry benchmarks — all in one place, built on top of our own live Shopify data.
