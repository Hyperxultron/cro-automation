Section 1 — Concept

What is CRO?

Conversion Rate Optimization is the practice of turning more of your existing visitors into paying customers — without spending more on ads.

Conversion Rate = Orders placed ÷ Store sessions. Every store leaks visitors at each step — session → added to cart → reached checkout → completed purchase. CRO means finding exactly where that leak happens, and fixing it.

2.5% average e-commerce conversion rate

Industry benchmark — most stores convert only 2–3 visitors out of every 100.

70% average cart abandonment rate

Baymard Institute — roughly 7 in 10 shoppers add to cart and leave without buying.
