Section 4 — Important to know

Which number uses which time window

Every card and chart already shows its own date range on screen — but here's the full picture in one place, since different metrics need different windows by nature.

MetricTime windowWhy
Revenue Today, AOV, Orders TodayToday (store timezone)Live snapshot of the current day
Conversion Funnel, Cart & CheckoutYesterday (full day)Shopify's own session analytics has a 1-day delay — today's data is incomplete until the day closes
Conversion Rate Trend chartLast 30 daysA trend needs a longer window to mean anything
Product AnalyticsSelectable: Today / 7 / 30 daysDepends what question you're asking
Order Health, Recommendations (refund/cancel checks)Last 30 daysEnough orders to avoid noisy, misleading percentages
Shopify CRO Automation 12
If asked "why is today's conversion rate not showing" — the honest answer is Shopify itself doesn't finalize session data until the day is over, so we show yesterday's complete, accurate number instead of a partial, misleading one.
